Our Story

Building healthier communities since 2005

What started as a small clinic with just five doctors has grown into one of the region's most trusted healthcare institutions. Today, MediCare Hospital is a modern, fully equipped medical center serving thousands of patients every year.

Our commitment to excellence has earned us accreditation from leading healthcare organizations and the trust of our community.

  • Accredited by Joint Commission International
  • ISO 9001:2015 certified for quality management
  • Partnered with top medical universities
  • Winner of Regional Healthcare Excellence Award 2024
